# Asset icon attribution

The brand paths embedded in components/asset-icon.tsx come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The upstream revision is pinned to 4ba19240849175ab4b855a732ab98c0f87cfb714, with selected icons from release 11.15.0.

Logos identify their respective companies; trademarks remain the property of their owners. JPMorgan Chase is represented by the Chase brand and Alphabet by Google. Alibaba uses the Alibaba.com brand. Source paths are monochrome and displayed in a brighter palette for legibility on dark backgrounds. All assets are embedded locally; no brand CDN is contacted at runtime.

Company monograms and fund/currency symbols are original interface artwork, not official logos.
